What we do

Bring your piece to the counter and Julio will look it over with you, explain what it needs, and give you a price before any work starts.

  • Chain, clasp and bracelet repair

    Broken links, worn clasps, snapped chains and bracelets that no longer close properly.

  • Ring resizing

    Rings made larger or smaller so they fit comfortably again.

  • Custom nameplates and pendants

    Names, initials and words in gold, in script or block letters, as necklaces or ID bracelets.

  • Cleaning, polishing and restoration

    Bringing back the shine on worn or tarnished pieces you want to keep wearing.

Not sure what your piece needs? Call the shop or bring it in.
